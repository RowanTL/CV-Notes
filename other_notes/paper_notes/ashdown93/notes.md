# Introduction

- In paper `A Study of Near-Field Indirect Lighting Calculations` by Mistrick and English [1990]
  - Before this paper
    - Which this paper attempts to improve upon
  - To summarize:
    - far-field data was used in near-field applications (bad)
      - Can't accurately model ceiling luminances near an indirect luminaire
        - Indirect luminaire meaning light reflected or scattered off 2+ surfaces
        - Direct luminaire light reflected off at most one surface into the camera
    - This paper wants to advance the state-of-the-art by investigating alternative measurement and calculation procedures from accurate near-field environment modeling
- Application Distance Photometry offers an approximation of near-field environment
